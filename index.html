About Us:

We are a friendly, reliable and dedicated family business. We have operated in Peterborough and its surrounding areas for the last 6 years. We use the latest reach and wash technology to clean in a careful and efficient way, all the way up to 4th story levels. We use pure RODI water which means there is no need to dry the windows after cleaning. The brushes we use are designed to get into every nook and cranny, deep cleaning the frames, ledges and surrounding plastics very thoroughly. At the same time, our brushes are elegant and soft, leaving no marks or scratches.
